# Reference: Minimalist Circular Avatar Features

This document provides a detailed breakdown of the visual features and design structure for the 6 minimalist circular face avatars arranged in a 2x3 grid.

---

## Global Design Attributes
- **Format:** Circular badge avatars, flat vector style.
- **Line Style:** Uniform monoline stroke (dark navy/slate outline).
- **Facial Proportions:** Simplified geometric shapes (circles, arcs, loops).
- **Backgrounds:** Solid pastel/medium-tone circular fills.

---

## Detailed Face Breakdown

### 1. Top-Left (Row 1, Column 1)
- **Background Color:** Neutral Cool Gray
- **Hair / Fringe:** 5 straight, vertical strokes across the top (comb/crew-cut fringe).
- **Eyebrows:** Short, gentle curved horizontal lines.
- **Eyes:** Simple hollow circular line outlines.
- **Nose:** Small, downward-curving horizontal arc.
- **Mouth:** Wide, open D-shaped smile (flat top line, curved bottom outline).
- **Expression:** Joyful, open, cheerful.

---

### 2. Top-Center (Row 1, Column 2)
- **Background Color:** Warm Sunshine Yellow
- **Hair / Fringe:** Single horizontal scalloped/wavy line across the forehead.
- **Eyebrows:** Flat, low horizontal strokes.
- **Eyes:** Simple hollow circular line outlines.
- **Nose:** Inverted U-shape / small central arc.
- **Mouth:** Wide, gentle upward single-line smile.
- **Expression:** Calm, friendly, contented.

---

### 3. Top-Right (Row 1, Column 3)
- **Background Color:** Coral / Peach Orange
- **Hair / Fringe:** Center-parted swooped fringe formed by two arched curves joining at a central peak.
- **Eyebrows:** None present.
- **Eyes:** Simple hollow circular line outlines.
- **Nose:** Small downward-curving arc.
- **Mouth & Chin:** Subtle upward smile stroke paired with a small secondary curve beneath to define the lower lip or chin indentation.
- **Expression:** Serene, relaxed, subtle smile.

---

### 4. Bottom-Left (Row 2, Column 1)
- **Background Color:** Emerald / Mint Green
- **Hair / Fringe:** Continuous jagged, zigzag horizontal fringe line.
- **Eyebrows:** Curved horizontal arches.
- **Eyes:** Simple hollow circular line outlines.
- **Nose:** Vertical elongated pill/loop shape.
- **Mouth:** Wide, horizontal rounded loop with a flattened bottom line.
- **Expression:** Playful, quirky, wide grin.

---

### 5. Bottom-Center (Row 2, Column 2)
- **Background Color:** Lavender / Periwinkle Blue-Purple
- **Hair / Fringe:** Smooth, multi-waved undulating crest line.
- **Eyebrows:** High, pronounced curved arches.
- **Eyes:** Looped spiral/curly strokes (snail-shell swirl style).
- **Nose:** Small downward-curving arc positioned low.
- **Mouth:** Open, rounded capsule/bean shape.
- **Expression:** Surprised, amazed, or singing.

---

### 6. Bottom-Right (Row 2, Column 3)
- **Background Color:** Cyan / Sky Blue
- **Hair / Fringe:** Asymmetric curved side-swept wave.
- **Eyebrows:** None present.
- **Eyes:** Simple hollow circular line outlines.
- **Cheeks:** 3 diagonal hatch/hash blush lines under each eye.
- **Nose:** Small downward-curving arc.
- **Mouth:** Small, open D-shaped happy smile.
- **Expression:** Shy, enthusiastic, blushing.
